## Draft Privacy Notice

Effective date: [only after approval]. Controller for KAVELYQ account and security information: [operator identity and address]. Contact: [privacy contact].

For appointment records, the salon shown on the booking page normally decides why customer data is collected and is the controller. KAVELYQ processes those records under that salon's instructions. Contact that salon first about an appointment, its cancellation policy or its customer-record retention. KAVELYQ assists the salon with requests concerning records it processes.

We process account email and business membership, business/staff/service configuration, customer name and supplied contact details, appointments and their status, customer notes, consent choices, waitlist requests, internal business recommendations, and limited audit/security metadata. Booking management links are private bearer credentials. Do not share them publicly. Do not put health information, identity documents, card details or other sensitive information into free-text notes during the pilot.

We use account information to provide and secure the requested service, administer access, communicate about service issues and maintain appropriate records. Depending on the purpose and applicable law, the operator relies on contract, legitimate interests in security/service administration, or a legal obligation. The salon determines and explains its own lawful basis for customer booking and service records. Optional marketing requires a separate appropriate basis; booking acceptance is not marketing consent. Imported customers do not receive marketing consent automatically.

The pilot does not collect payments, enable advertising campaigns or send automated customer marketing. Any future transactional email processing must be disclosed and separately configured before activation. We do not promise email delivery merely because an appointment is confirmed or a job is queued. Rules-based intelligence uses recorded business data and does not make unreviewed consequential decisions or send outreach.

Providers supporting the service include Supabase for database/authentication and Vercel for hosting/content delivery. Google Fonts requests may disclose network metadata to the font provider. Resend is a proposed transactional email provider and must appear in the final subprocessor list only with its actual activated scope and terms. Review provider locations, retention, security and any relevant international transfer mechanisms before approving real data processing. No sale of customer lists or advertising tracking is part of the proposed pilot.

Proposed retention schedule, awaiting approval and operational implementation: customer/booking records for the salon's documented necessary period; pilot end export followed by deletion within [30 days], subject to documented lawful retention; security/audit metadata [90 days]; delivery diagnostics [30 days]; encrypted backup rotation [30 days]. These periods are proposals, not current automated behavior. Suppression records need a minimal retention basis to avoid contacting opted-out recipients. Where erasure applies, restored backups must replay the deletion ledger before returning to service.

People may have rights to access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and may withdraw consent for optional processing. Send requests to [contact] or the relevant salon. We verify the requester proportionately and respond within applicable statutory deadlines, normally one month subject to lawful exceptions/extensions. Complaints may be made to the competent supervisory authority. The operator's establishment, UK targeting and representation obligations must be assessed before naming a lead authority.

We use access controls, tenant permissions, encrypted transport and other documented safeguards. No system is risk-free. We do not claim production backups, monitoring, email delivery or uptime capabilities until they have been configured and verified. Material changes to this notice will be communicated before taking effect where required.

## Draft Cookie and Browser Storage Disclosure

The current application uses browser sessionStorage for Owner access/refresh sessions, demo state, navigation and booking state. It is scoped to the browser context and does not carry an Owner session automatically from Gmail's embedded browser into Safari. Refresh within the same tab can retain the session; closing a tab/private-browsing context or clearing storage may require a fresh sign-in. Guest contact information is not intentionally stored in persistent localStorage; private management links must still be protected.

No advertising, analytics or cross-site marketing cookies have been added for this launch preparation. Provider pages and authentication redirects may have their own necessary storage. Google Fonts and approved external business imagery cause network requests and must be disclosed. The final deployment needs a fresh storage/network inventory before publication. Do not add a cosmetic consent banner claiming consent for trackers that are not present. Any optional analytics/tracking added later needs a purpose, retention and appropriate consent assessment first.

## Draft Data Processing Addendum

Controller: [salon]. Processor: [operator]. Subject matter: provision of agreed appointment/business management services. Duration: agreement term plus a documented return/deletion period. Nature: storage, retrieval, scheduling, updating, export and erasure on documented instructions. Purpose: salon appointment administration and approved customer management. Data subjects: salon clients, staff and authorised business users. Data: the categories in the privacy draft; special-category data is excluded from the pilot.

The processor shall:
1. Process only documented lawful controller instructions, including international transfer instructions; notify the controller if an instruction appears to infringe applicable data protection law.
2. Ensure authorised persons owe confidentiality and have appropriate access. Apply tenant isolation, least privilege, secure transport, secret management and the security/recovery procedures in the operational annex; do not promise unimplemented controls.
3. Engage only authorised subprocessors under equivalent written obligations, maintain a current list and agreed change/objection process. Initial proposed list: Supabase (database/auth), Vercel (hosting), and Resend only if email is activated. Confirm contracting entities, locations and transfer safeguards before signature.
4. Assist proportionately with data-subject rights, security, breach notification, DPIAs and regulator consultations, considering processing scope and available information.
5. Notify the controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach, providing facts and updates as available. Do not wait for a final investigation or treat the controller's possible 72-hour regulatory deadline as a processor waiting period.
6. At the controller's choice return or delete personal data on termination, including existing copies unless legally required to retain them. Explain backup expiry, access restrictions and deletion replay on restoration.
7. Make compliance information available and permit agreed proportionate audits/inspections. Record processing/subprocessor/incident activities as required.
8. Not use customer records for its own marketing, unrelated model training or independent profiling without a separately assessed lawful basis and transparent agreement.

The controller warrants authorised instructions, lawful collection and adequate notices, and identifies personnel authorised to request exports, erasure or configuration changes. Include allocation of costs, assistance, liability and disputes only after professional review. The processing and security annex must match the actual deployed configuration.
